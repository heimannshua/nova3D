## Intent

**Problem:** A person cannot start a Project. Nothing lets them describe a model, see what nova3D understood, correct it and confirm it, so nothing records the exact subject, scope, outcome and personalization that research and generation will later be held to. The domain-package manifest and registry that say which subjects nova3D can build do not exist either.

**Approach:** Add the domain-package manifest contract and registry port (the Middot manifest holding its one registered subject), a deterministic and free understanding step (match the description against registered subject names and aliases, map personalization wording to the one supported kind), the immutable request-revision record family that Projects own, one idempotent command that creates the Project and its first confirmed revision at confirmation, and the screens C-01 (made real for text), C-02 and C-06. Confirming a request starts nothing, approves nothing and spends nothing.

## Boundaries & Constraints

No new npm dependency, environment variable or secret, and no network or billable call anywhere in this story. **Manifest and registry:** `packages/contracts/domain-manifest.ts` (no runtime imports, erasable syntax only) holds `DomainManifest = {packageId, version, subjects}`, `ManifestSubject = {id, displayName, aliases}` and `validateManifest(value)`, which names every problem and never throws. Rules: `packageId` and subject `id` match `^[a-z][a-z0-9-]{0,62}$`; `version` is a positive integer; 1 to 32 subjects; `displayName` is trimmed, 1 to 120 characters, no control character; 1 to 16 aliases of 1 to 60 characters, trimmed, no control character, and no two aliases of one subject equal after normalization. `lib/domain-registry.ts` holds the port `DomainRegistry {subjects(): readonly RegisteredSubject[]; subject(id): RegisteredSubject | null}` (registration order; `RegisteredSubject` is the subject plus `packageId` and `packageVersion`), `createDomainRegistry(manifests)` (throws a `TypeError` naming the problems when a manifest is invalid or a subject ID repeats; two subjects MAY share an alias) and `defaultDomainRegistry`, which registers Middot only. `packages/domains/middot/manifest.ts`: package `middot`, version 1, one subject `middot-altar-ramp`, display name "The outer altar and ramp (Middot 3)", aliases exactly `altar`, `ramp`, `mizbeach`, `kevesh`, `מזבח`, `כבש`. The alias list is not approved yet: Josh approves it with the G-1 corpus, recorded in `docs/domain-packages.md` and `deferred-work.md`, and no code gates on it. The fixture second subject (`fixture-incense-altar`, "The incense altar (fixture)", aliases `altar`, `incense`) lives in `tests/support/` only and is never in the default registry. **Understanding** (`lib/request-intake.ts`, pure, client-safe, `import type` only, no `process`, no I/O): `normalizeText` is NFKC, then removal of the Hebrew points U+0591 to U+05C7 except U+05BE, U+05C0, U+05C3 and U+05C6 (those four become a space), then lower case, then every whitespace run one space, then trim. A subject matches when the normalized description contains one of its normalized aliases as a whole word: the character before and the character after are not a letter, digit or mark (`\p{L}\p{N}\p{M}`); an alias that starts with a Hebrew letter also matches after one or two of the prefix letters ו ב כ ל מ ש ה that themselves follow a non-letter (so `המזבח`, `במזבח` and `לכבש` match; `altarpiece` and `ramps` do not). A description is 1 to 2,000 characters after trimming, with no control character but newline and tab. `understand` returns exactly one of `invalid` (named field problems), `no_match` (the registered subjects, for the explanation), `ambiguous` (the matching subjects, in registry order) or `clear` (one subject); a chosen subject is accepted only if it is among the matches. Scope and outcome are free text of 1 to 300 characters after trimming that the person edits; the screen prefills scope with the subject's display name and outcome with a localized catalog sentence, and the server only validates them. **Personalization** (`classifyPersonalization(wording, mode)`): empty wording is `none`; mode `image_direct` declines any wording with reason `image_direct_has_no_surfaces`; otherwise the wording (at most 300 characters) must contain exactly one quoted text, written `"…"`, `“…”` or `«…»` (none: `no_quoted_text`; more than one: `several_texts`), the text trimmed, 1 to 24 characters (`text_too_long`), no control character (`text_invalid`); the words outside the quotes decide the style: recessed (`recessed`, `engraved`, `carved`, `incised`, `debossed`, `sunken`, `חרוט`, `שקוע`), raised (`raised`, `embossed`, `relief`, `protruding`, `בולט`, `מוגבה`), both is `conflicting_styles`, neither is `raised`. Everything else in the wording is not used, and the screen says so. A declined wish is explained in plain words, is still allowed to be confirmed, and is recorded not applied with its reason. The 24-character limit is the first surface's (Story 4.2) and moves into the surface declaration there. **Records** (migration `20261018120000_request_revisions.sql`, `projects` schema, closed like `project_commands`: RLS on, no policy, every privilege revoked, reached only through service-role `api.*` functions that are `security definer` with `search_path = ''`): `projects.request_revisions` (id; `project_id` and `account_id` with a restricted FK to `projects(id, account_id)`; `seq` at least 1, unique per Project; `command_id`, unique per Account; `mode` in `evidence_text|evidence_images|image_direct`; `subject_id` matching the slug rule, required when the mode is `evidence_text`; `scope`; `outcome`; `description`; `personalization_wording`, `_text`, `_style` (`raised|recessed`), `_applied`, `_reason`; `digest`; `confirmed_at`; `created_at`) with checks that applied means text and style are set and the mode is not `image_direct`, not applied means text and style are null, and a reason is present exactly when a wording exists and is not applied. A row is never updated and the table never truncated; deleting is left for the Epic 8 purge, so the FK restricts and the cleanup helpers delete it first. The Project's stage check gains `request_confirmed`. `api.create_project_request(account, epoch, command_id, title, mode, subject_id, scope, outcome, description, personalization fields)` calls `identity.assert_operation_authority` first (N3010 is `denied`), takes the advisory lock `'project_command:'||account||':'||command`, refuses a command ID that a rename already used, replays the original receipt for the same command and the same digest (`replayed`), answers `reused` for the same command with another digest, and otherwise creates in one transaction the Project (title, stage `request_confirmed`, revision 1, in the Account's Workspace) and its request revision 1, answering `created` with both IDs. `digest` is SHA-256 over the length-prefixed fields (title included). Invalid shape raises 22023; domain results are returned. `api.read_project_request(account, epoch, project_id)` answers `denied`, `unavailable` (unknown, another Account's, deleted or tombstoned: one answer), `none` (a Project without a revision) or `found` with the latest revision's fields. ## Spec Change Log

## Design Notes

Nothing is stored until Confirm: matching is free and deterministic, so the understanding is recomputed from the submitted fields every time, and abandoned drafts, a title before the subject is known and a Project in a stage that is not true all disappear. The revision doubles as the command receipt (`command_id` is unique per Account), so there is one row to read and one place for the digest. Successors (Stories 2.2, 2.3, 2.12, 2.13) append revisions with `seq + 1` to an existing Project, hold the Project row, and raise its revision by one; this story creates revision 1 together with the Project at revision 1.

The Hebrew prefix rule departs from a plain "whole word" because a Hebrew article or preposition is written attached to its noun; without it `המזבח` would not find `מזבח`. The stored quote of the person's own words (`description`, `personalization_wording`) is never altered; only comparisons use the normalized form.

```
"Please build the altar and ramp, and add "David" engraved on the base"
  -> clear: middot-altar-ramp; scope "The outer altar and ramp (Middot 3)"; personalization text "David", recessed
"add something nice"  -> declined: no_quoted_text   (confirm allowed; stored not applied)
```
