The material actually used is written into the Source Record, so a reader knows what the printed object is made of.
Filament awareness
Colour is a swatch and a colour name; type is always a word, never a colour code. An unidentified spool says so and states the consequence — nova3D will not check it against a slice — rather than guessing. Mismatch separates the consequential difference (type, affecting temperatures) from the cosmetic one (colour), and the material used ends up in the Source Record.

Icons
Section headings, objects and actions only. Genuine Phosphor duotone paths, inlined on currentColor, aria-hidden, always beside a text label — no icon is the sole carrier of meaning, and none depends on a network request.
