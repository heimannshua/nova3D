New scope: nova3D goes the whole way to the printer and keeps watching

Scope change recorded. The handoff said nova3D never slices and never controls a printer. Per your direction it now prepares the model, arranges the build plate, slices, and sends the job to a vendor-linked printer. Three rules hold that together, and every screen below obeys them: nova3D auto-fixes printing-only problems (orientation, supports, brim, plate position) without asking; it never alters reconstruction geometry on its own; and where printability and an evidence-backed dimension collide it asks every time. Printers are found automatically, controlled from nova3D (pause, resume, stop), watched through their camera, and a print that looks wrong is paused with a Notification saying what nova3D saw and how to fix it — always with resume offered, because camera detection can be wrong. Slice figures, times and material amounts read “verified content pending” rather than invented numbers.

What nova3D saw
The front left corner of the base has risen away from the plate over the last 16 layers. The nozzle is now printing onto a surface that is no longer flat.
Why this happens
The plastic shrinks as it cools and pulls the corner up. It is most common on a small footprint like this one, and on a cool or dusty plate.
How to fix it now
1Press the lifted corner back down while the plate is still warm.
2If it will not stay, stop the print — it will not recover on its own.
3Resume only once the corner is flat again.
Next time
nova3D added a brim to this print already. A wider brim, a clean plate, or a warmer first layer would help. It can apply those when you print this again.
