A print plate is a physical record of how an image was made, which is the product's argument in one image. The plate wordmark and numerals give nova3D a mark of its own, the proof margin turns global status into press furniture at the sheet edge, and the halftone frame keeps preview geometry visibly a preview rather than authoritative manufacturing geometry.

Accessibility

Plate text needs care: the plate repeats are aria-hidden and the paper layer carries the real string, so screen readers hear the wordmark once. The plate fringe reduces effective contrast, so plates are display-only — never body copy, never a control label — and must have a flat fallback under high contrast and forced colours. Step blocks give progress a countable shape as well as a colour. Reduced motion turns the plate drift off entirely.

Trade-offs

Strongest identity and the highest chance of feeling decorative next to serious evidence work; the plate treatment must never touch a Detail Status or an approval gate. Dark mode needs the numerals to keep their own paper patch, which is a real constraint rather than a token swap. Heavier to build and to keep accessible than 1a or 1b.
