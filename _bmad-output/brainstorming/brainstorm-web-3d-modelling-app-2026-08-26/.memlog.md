- (direction by user) Use Vercel, Supabase, Upstash, GitHub, React, and Tailwind as the intended web stack
- (decision by coach) Technique sequence: First Principles Thinking, SCAMPER Method, One Feature Only, Sci-Fi Artifact From the Future
- (technique by coach) started First Principles Thinking
- (direction by user) First-time developer who needs implementation guidance and an achievable initial product scope
- (direction by user) The app is intended only for the user, not for customers or teams
- (insight by coach) Personal-only scope can eliminate billing, collaboration, marketplace, broad onboarding, and complex permissions
- (direction by user) Generate many kinds of precise models whose creation requires deep research, then sell the resulting models
- (idea by coach) A private research-to-model pipeline that gathers evidence, turns it into geometry requirements, and produces commercially saleable 3D assets
- (insight by coach) Research provenance and precision verification may be as important as the modelling interface
- (idea by user) First model collection: the Jewish Temple and its sacred utensils
- (insight by coach) Historically grounded reconstruction must represent disagreement between textual, archaeological, and scholarly sources rather than claiming false certainty
- (idea by coach) Evidence-aware reconstruction workspace linking each model dimension and design choice to a source, interpretation, and confidence level
- (decision by user) The initial reconstruction domain is the Second Temple and its utensils
- (insight by coach) A precise Second Temple reconstruction requires choosing a dated historical phase because the complex changed over centuries
- (decision by user) Base the Second Temple reconstruction primarily on Mishnah tractate Middot
- (idea by coach) Treat tractate Middot as the governing spatial specification and visibly label details supplemented or inferred from other sources
- (decision by user) The app should automatically generate the reconstruction from tractate Middot rather than rely on manual modelling
- (idea by coach) Source-to-geometry pipeline: parse passages into spatial claims, expose ambiguities, convert approved claims into parametric geometry, and retain passage links on every object
- (direction by user) When Middot is ambiguous, research books and use their arguments to determine which interpretation to model
- (idea by coach) Research adjudicator: retrieve relevant commentaries, compare interpretations, recommend a position with citations and confidence, then bind the selected position to geometry
- (decision by user) The app must wait for user approval before applying a recommended interpretation
- (idea by coach) Approval ledger records the chosen interpretation, rejected alternatives, citations, confidence, approver, and the geometry affected
- (decision by user) Review disputed interpretations as a grouped set rather than interrupting generation for each detail
- (idea by coach) Section-level decision dossier batches disputed claims, competing sources, recommended positions, and predicted geometry effects for approval
- (technique by coach) started SCAMPER Method: Substitute
- (direction by user) Use concrete beginner-friendly language and explain modelling or development jargon
- (decision by user) All geometry and modelling work should be generated automatically; the user should not manually construct the 3D model
- (insight by coach) The user role is research approval and creative direction, while the system owns complete geometry generation
- (technique by coach) SCAMPER switched to Combine
- (decision by user) Generate only the 3D model; do not generate images, videos, explanations, or product listings as separate deliverables
- (technique by coach) SCAMPER switched to Adapt
- (idea by coach) Version every approved model state so interpretations can be compared and earlier geometry restored
- (decision by user) Saved model versions would be useful
- (technique by coach) SCAMPER switched to Modify
- (decision by user) Changing an approved measurement should automatically rebuild every connected part affected by it
- (idea by coach) Dependency-aware regeneration updates connected geometry after a source claim or measurement changes and saves the result as a new version
- (technique by coach) SCAMPER switched to Put-to-other-use
- (decision by user) The same system should later accept different books and research topics to generate other kinds of accurate models
- (insight by coach) Build a reusable source-to-model engine, with tractate Middot implemented as the first domain package rather than hard-coding the whole application
- (technique by coach) SCAMPER switched to Eliminate
- (idea by coach) Automated print pipeline validates geometry, prepares it for the selected printer, and submits the job after user confirmation
- (technique by coach) SCAMPER switched to Reverse
- (decision by user) Eliminate manual modelling because the purpose is automatic research and construction
- (direction by user) Connect directly to personal 3D printers so a generated model can be printed immediately
- (direction by user) Support multiple connected 3D printers with detailed device information
- (decision by user) Never interrupt an active print; queue the new job when a suitable printer is unavailable
- (direction by user) Track the colour and material type of filament currently loaded in each printer
- (idea by coach) Printer fleet manager matches jobs to printer availability, build dimensions, capabilities, and loaded filament, then maintains a safe queue
- (decision by user) Automatically choose the best printer based on model requirements, loaded filament, availability, and queue time
- (direction by user) Allow printer settings to be controlled from the app
- (direction by user) Connect to printer cameras for live monitoring
- (idea by coach) Unified printer console exposes fleet status, job queues, print settings, automatic routing, filament state, and camera feeds
- (technique by coach) started One Feature Only
- (decision by user) The indispensable first capability is deep research combined with automatic 3D generation
- (insight by coach) Treat research and generation as one traceable end-to-end capability because research without geometry and geometry without evidence both fail the goal
- (decision by user) Show the supporting sources for every generated feature and modelling decision
- (idea by coach) Evidence graph links each object, measurement, material, and placement to exact source passages, competing interpretations, and the approved decision
- (decision by user) Clicking a model part should reveal its exact sources, alternatives, confidence, and approved interpretation
- (technique by coach) started Sci-Fi Artifact From the Future
- (idea by user) The finished experience presents a completed model file that is ready to print
- (insight by coach) Hide modelling and file-format complexity; deliver a validated print-ready file as the visible outcome
- (decision by user) Inspect the completed model before it is sent to a printer
- (idea by coach) Read-only 3D inspection stage supports rotate, zoom, section hiding, and evidence inspection without exposing manual modelling tools
- (decision by user) During inspection, clicking a questionable part should trigger focused re-research, approval, and regeneration of affected geometry
- (idea by coach) Closed-loop correction workflow turns visual inspection into targeted research revision without manual geometry editing
- (insight by user) All parts of the concept matter because the intended outcome must be completely accurate
- (insight by coach) The product is one integrity chain from source quality through interpretation approval, geometry generation, print validation, and physical output
- (insight by coach) Where historical sources are incomplete or disputed, replace impossible certainty with complete traceability: explicitly mark every detail as sourced, inferred, disputed, or unknown and never silently invent
